Top Tools to Make Money

Affiliate picks — I earn a commission if you sign up through my links at no extra cost to you.

Building a side income online starts with the right tools and realistic expectations. Below is a breakdown of the most accessible ways to begin, what each one actually pays, and how long it typically takes to see your first dollar.

  • Freelance Writing: Write blog posts, product descriptions, and newsletters for small businesses. Beginners typically start at $25–$50 per article and can reach $150+ per piece within a few months. You don't need a portfolio — you need one strong sample and the courage to send cold emails.
  • Email Marketing: Build a mailing list and promote products or your own services. Most creators earn anywhere from $1 to $5 per subscriber per month. A list of 1,000 engaged subscribers can realistically generate $1,000–$5,000/month in affiliate and product revenue.
  • Digital Templates: Create and sell Notion trackers, Excel budget spreadsheets, Canva planners, and PDF guides on Etsy or Gumroad. These are "build once, sell forever" products. Successful template sellers commonly make $500–$3,000/month in passive income after the initial setup.
  • Side-Hustle Blueprint: A step-by-step method to go from zero to your first $1,000 online: pick one service, find three paying clients, deliver excellent work, and reinvest your earnings into tools that save time. Most people who follow this consistently reach $1,000 within 8–12 weeks.

Consistency beats intensity. Pick one method, take action daily for 90 days, and reinvest your early earnings into scaling what works. The people who succeed online aren't necessarily the most talented — they're the ones who didn't quit in week three.
